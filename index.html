Before you scan

Before you scan: Handwritten recipes are not recommended for scanning. They are hard to read, so we recommend typing those in by hand instead. For best results, all photos need good lighting and a well-framed, straight-on shot of the full recipe. Scanning is never 100% accurate, even with a clear photo, so please always proofread the scanned recipe carefully before saving it to be completely sure it is correct.
