Cook Week

Plan what you'll cook each day, meal by meal — every day has a Breakfast, a Lunch and a Supper slot, and you can add as many recipes to each slot as you like. So Tuesday could be eggs for breakfast, chicken at lunch and chili for supper. Tap the circle when you've made one (tap again to undo it), and tap a recipe's name — or its View recipe button — to open it. Use Portions and Save to size a recipe up or down for that meal: the badge by its name shows the multiple, and the recipe opens already at that size.

Before you scan

Before you scan: Handwritten recipes are not recommended for scanning. They are hard to read, so we recommend typing those in by hand instead. For best results, all photos need good lighting and a well-framed, straight-on shot of the full recipe. Scanning is never 100% accurate, even with a clear photo, so please always proofread the scanned recipe carefully before saving it to be completely sure it is correct.
